          How this works

          Saving progress

          Your six-digit passcode loads the shared online ranking once the Cloudflare save API is deployed.

          Choosing countries

          Pick the country you prefer. Hard to choose gives both a small boost. Skip changes the pair without changing rankings.

          Partner rankings

          Love and removal

          The heart saves a country to your own loved list. Suggest removal asks the other person to agree before that country leaves future matchups.
